# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and technically specific.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them promptly.
- Focus discussion on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy, copyright, credentials, and responsible crawling practices.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, publication of private
information, or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate is not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the project maintainer through the contact method in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove content or temporarily or permanently
exclude contributors when necessary to protect the community.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
